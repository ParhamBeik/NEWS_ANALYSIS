# Reader release checklist

This is an implementation guide, not evidence that the reader is ready for rollout.

1. Apply Django migrations in News Intelligence and run `seed_sources`. The registry currently has **14 feed channels**, including the original five Iranian crawlers and nine official international channels. Each added official endpoint was checked for HTTP 200, parseable XML, and recent entries on 2026-09-29. The first-wave goal remains 25 independently verified channels. Source images stay hidden until `public_image_allowed` is reviewed per source.
2. Configure `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` on the News backend/worker. Jev uses the pinned `typesafe/jev-1.13` Decisions API. The brief uses `OPENROUTER_BRIEF_MODEL` (default `openai/gpt-6-luna`). Keep `NEWS_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD=100`; the Redis budget reserves capacity before calls and records actual reported costs by stage. No credential belongs in this file.
3. Configure `NEWS_MARKET_SERVICE_KEY` on the Portfolio backend and set the same value as `PORTFOLIO_MARKET_SERVICE_KEY` on the News backend. Set `PORTFOLIO_MARKET_BASE_URL` to the Portfolio service's approved internal origin. The warehouse endpoint serves only `tehran_index`, `bitcoin`, and `oil`; it never exposes holdings or user price endpoints. Verify actual series, unit, provider, gaps, and closures before showing each asset publicly.
4. Configure `NEWS_VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `NEWS_VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY`, and `NEWS_VAPID_SUBJECT` for browser push. Keep `NEWS_ALERTS_ENABLED=false` during shadow scoring. Staff record Jev review labels in Django admin under Event reviews. Enable delivery only after the reviewed sample meets the alert gate.
5. Run `manage.py reader_release_gate`. It requires 25 enabled sources with a successful crawl in the last six hours, 100 reviewed cases including at least 10 top-tier cases, 100 priority-source observations, p95 visibility at most 10 minutes, false top-tier alerts at most 2%, missed top-tier events at most 5%, and monthly AI spend at most $100. These are initial acceptance thresholds; the reviewed sample may justify adjusting them before rollout. Check the `/ops` source outage and latency panels as well.

The official feed paths were taken from the [Federal Reserve feed directory](https://www.federalreserve.gov/feeds/feeds.htm), [ECB feed directory](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/home/html/rss.en.html), [EIA feed directory](https://www.eia.gov/tools/rssfeeds/), and [BEA developer resources](https://www.bea.gov/resources/for-developers). The BLS feed returned HTTP 403 from this environment and was not added. [OFAC retired its RSS feed in 2025](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20250206); its recent-actions page needs a separate extractor. Bloomberg, Reuters, Forbes, and AP distribution require separate licensing and are not enabled.
